What are ADMET properties?
ADMET stands for Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion, and Toxicity. These properties describe how a drug behaves in the human body and are crucial for drug development and safety assessment.
How accurate are the predictions?
The predictions are based on machine learning models trained on experimental data. While they provide valuable insights for drug discovery, they should always be validated with experimental studies before making critical decisions.
Can I search by SMILES notation?
Yes, you can search compounds using their SMILES notation in addition to compound names. The app will recognize both formats and retrieve the corresponding compound data.
What databases are used?
The app utilizes PubChem for compound information and molecular properties, ADMET-AI for pharmacokinetic predictions, and ProTox for toxicity analysis. All data is cross-validated for accuracy.
How do I interpret the scores?
ADMET scores range from 0-100, where higher values indicate better drug-like properties. Green indicates favorable properties, yellow suggests moderate concerns, and red indicates potential issues that may need attention.
Is my search data stored?
No, all compound searches and results are processed locally and not stored on our servers. Your research data remains private and secure.

🧬 About ChemADMET

ChemADMET is a comprehensive mobile application designed for researchers, pharmacologists, and drug developers to quickly assess the pharmacokinetic and toxicological properties of chemical compounds.

📊 Key Features

  • Compound Search: Access to extensive PubChem database with detailed molecular information
  • ADMET Analysis: AI-powered predictions for Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion, and Toxicity
  • Toxicity Assessment: Comprehensive safety evaluation including mutagenicity and carcinogenicity
  • Mobile Optimized: Designed specifically for 9:16 portrait mobile interfaces
  • Scientific Accuracy: Based on validated machine learning models and experimental data

🔬 Data Sources

Our application integrates data from multiple trusted scientific databases:

  • PubChem: Compound identification and molecular properties
  • ADMET-AI: Pharmacokinetic property predictions
  • ProTox: Toxicity and safety assessments
  • ChEMBL: Bioactivity data validation

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This application provides computational predictions for research purposes only. Results should not be used as a substitute for experimental validation or clinical testing. Always consult with qualified professionals for drug development decisions.
